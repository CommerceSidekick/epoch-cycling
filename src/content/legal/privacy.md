## 1. Scope

This policy covers epochcycling.com, its forms, and orders of Epoch supplements (Epoch Endurance, Epoch Fuel and Epoch Edge) and kit. The Epoch app is covered by the Epoch Health System privacy policy (linked below). This site never collects clinical information. Any clinical information is handled on the Epoch Health System side under the clinical provider's own notice.

## 3. What we collect, form by form

**Join the club (waitlist).** First name, email, and optionally your ZIP code, what you ride and what you're in for. A check that you're 18 or older. Your email consent, with the time and the exact consent text.

**Riders Club application.** Name, email, optional phone, city and state, the role you're applying for, what you race or ride, your license type, how many races you plan, optional race calendar, results link, social handle and follower range, whether you'd host a ride, jersey size, and what you tell us about yourself. Your attestations (18 or older, terms read, disclosure, anti-doping responsibility) and optional email consent.

**Contact.** Name, email, topic, optional race or event name, and your message. App, account and privacy messages are passed to Epoch Health System support.

**Footer signup.** Email and your email consent.

**Race claims (later).** Race name and date, results link, post link, entry receipt, kit photo, entry amount and payment preference. Photo location data is removed on upload. <span class="ph" data-placeholder>[counsel: confirm before claims open]</span>

**From your visit.** Campaign tags in the link you used (for example utm_source), if any.
