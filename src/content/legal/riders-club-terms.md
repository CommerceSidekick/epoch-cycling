## 7. Disclosure (FTC)

Every post, story, video or review about Epoch must clearly disclose the relationship: #ad or #sponsored plus #EpochAthlete at the start, or the platform's paid-partnership tool, and spoken disclosure in video. #EpochRidersClub alone is not a disclosure. Riders may not make claims about Epoch products beyond what Epoch publishes, and may not make any health or disease claims.

## 8. Ambassadors

Ambassador codes and any commissions apply only to Epoch supplements (Epoch Endurance, Epoch Fuel and Epoch Edge) and kit. Never to the app's paid tiers or coaching, and never to any medical program or medical service, under any circumstances. Ambassadors may not promote, refer people to, or post about any medical program as part of the program.

## 9. No medical tie

Membership, selection, renewal and claims never depend on, and are never affected by, use of the app's paid plans, coaching, or any medical program or therapy. Epoch does not provide, supply, recommend or encourage any substance on the WADA Prohibited List.

## 10. Anti-doping

Club racers are solely responsible for complying with the anti-doping rules that apply to them (for example USA Cycling, UCI, USADA and WADA rules), including checking the Prohibited List and obtaining any required Therapeutic Use Exemption before use. A rider who receives an anti-doping sanction is removed from the program. Repayment of past reimbursements: <span class="ph" data-placeholder>[rule]</span>. Epoch makes no claim that its products are free of prohibited substances unless and until they are third-party certified, and even then the rider remains responsible.

## 11. Conduct and removal

Be decent, follow event and trail rules, no harassment, no unsafe riding. Epoch may remove any member at its discretion.
