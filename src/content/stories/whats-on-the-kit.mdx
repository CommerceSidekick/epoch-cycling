We wanted a kit you'd wear even if you'd never heard of us. So we kept it clean.

## EPOCH on the chest.

That's the crew. Same word on every kit, every discipline.

## Your discipline on the sleeve.

MTB, GRAVEL or ROAD. Same club, different dirt.

<KitFigure caption="Illustrations. Final kit after design sign-off." />

## Three marks on the other sleeve.

No words. Just three small icons, always in the same order.

<KitMark name="ride" title="Ride: the paceline" color="var(--color-summit)">
  Three chevrons in a line. The one up front is solid. The two behind are tucked in. Nobody gets fast alone, and the person in front takes the wind for everyone else.
</KitMark>

<KitMark name="fuel" title="Fuel: the horizon drop" color="var(--color-sage)">
  A single drop, cut by a line. That line sits at the same height as the bar in the E of EPOCH. Fuel below the horizon, ready for the climb.
</KitMark>

<KitMark name="optimize" title="Optimize: the ridge line" color="var(--color-sky)">
  A ride profile that keeps climbing, three steps up to a summit dot. Every season a little higher.
</KitMark>

## One more thing.

Club racers get a single stripe on the left cuff in Signal, our highlighter color. If you see it at the start line, say hi. That rider's racing for the crew.

And inside the collar, small enough that only you know it's there: NOW IS YOUR TIME.
